SoundByte — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

SoundByte is an Android app for turning video into MP3, trimming audio and merging audio clips. It is developed and published by RJ Digital Solutions Ltd, Northern Ireland, UK ("we", "us"). This policy explains exactly what happens to your data when you use the app.

1. Files you process

SoundByte converts files on our processing server, not on your phone. When you tap Extract, Trim or Merge, the file or files you picked are sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to our server. For a trim, the start and end times you chose are sent as well. The server is hosted by Render Services, Inc. in the United States (Oregon).

Your files are used only to create the MP3 you asked for. They are held in temporary storage while they are processed and deleted automatically as soon as the MP3 has been sent back to your phone, or straight away if processing fails. If the server restarts during a job, its temporary storage is wiped. We do not keep copies, we do not listen to or review your files, and we do not link them to you. SoundByte has no accounts, so files are never tied to a name, email address or profile.

2. Technical information

Like any internet service, our server and our hosting provider may record basic technical details of each connection, such as the IP address it came from, the time and whether it succeeded. These records are used only to keep the service running and secure, are not used to identify you, and are kept only for a short period by our hosting provider.

3. What stays on your phone

The MP3s you create, and the list shown in My Files, are stored in SoundByte's own storage on your phone. They stay there until you delete them or uninstall the app. You choose when to share an MP3 with another app.

4. Permissions

SoundByte does not ask for access to your photos, videos, music or microphone. You pick files with Android's built-in file picker, which gives the app only the files you choose. The app uses internet access to reach our processing server and Google Play.

5. In-app purchase

The optional one-time Pro upgrade is sold and processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy, and we never see or store them. The app receives only confirmation from Google Play that Pro has been bought, so it can unlock Pro features.

6. No ads, analytics or tracking

SoundByte has no advertising and no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell or share your data. Render processes files only on our behalf, as our hosting provider.

7. Your rights

Because files are deleted once processing finishes and the app has no accounts, we normally hold no data about you. Under UK data protection law you can still ask us what we hold, or ask us to delete it, by emailing the address below.

8. Children

SoundByte is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how SoundByte handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.

10. Contact

For any questions, email admin@rjdigitalsolutions.co.uk.
